rdui

A sandbox for reaction-diffusion, running live on your GPU.

Run reaction-diffusion and related field rules, paint into them, tune them as they run, and collect what they grow. Rules are written in Ready’s formula language, and Ready patterns open directly.

Open rdui Source on GitHub

Needs a browser with WebGPU: recent Chrome or Edge, or Safari 26. On Linux, Chrome may need --enable-unsafe-webgpu --enable-features=Vulkan.

What it does

Every picture on this page is a live simulation, recorded straight from the app.

Run

Over 50 built-in rules and more than 100 ready-made scenes: Gray-Scott, smoke, phase-field bubbles, Schrödinger waves, solitons and gliders.

Paint

Seed, erase, draw walls and paint parameters into the field while it runs.

Explore

See variants of a rule side by side, or a whole map of its parameters, and tap the one you like.

Colour

Every reagent gets its own colour. 🎲 rolls a new palette fitted to what's on screen.
